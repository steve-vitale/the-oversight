NARR: "About what you did with your hands — You look handy. What did you do with your hands, before?"
YOU: (tell them your trade)
NARR: "Useful. Everything useful matters now. I mean that as a compliment."
YOU: "..."
NARR: The silence stretches a beat too long. They study their hands.
YOU: "Yeah?"
NARR: The air goes tight. Their eyes narrow, just slightly.
YOU: "Wait — what were we talking about?"
NARR: "Still on what you did with your hands."
YOU: "Can I ask you something else?"
NARR: "Sorry — I shouldn't load that on you. What's up?"
YOU: "What are you good at?"
NARR: "Good question." "I was a librarian. Which means I'm good at taking things apart to see how they work."
YOU: "Tell me more."
NARR: "Learned it in Kinshasa, DR Congo, mostly. Librarian work teaches you fast — the kind that doesn't wait while you read a manual."
YOU: (listen)
NARR: "What I'm bad at? letting things go. We all get one fatal flaw. That's mine."
YOU: "Here, I know this one — let me show you."
NARR: You show them Wood Sorrel — where it grows, how to tell it apart. They nod slowly, committing it to memory. "Good to know. Really good to know."
YOU: "Show me sometime?"
NARR: "I've got things to see to. Don't be a stranger."
YOU: "..."
NARR: "You're a good listener, you know that?" A tired smile.
NARR: "...Sorry, lost my train of thought."
