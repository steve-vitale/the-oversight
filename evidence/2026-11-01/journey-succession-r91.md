## MEASUREMENT CAVEAT: same-iteration double handoff collapses

28152's chainDeaths list has 7 entries, but the village truly
changed bearers 9 times (Patrick + 8 successors). On day 71 two
handoffs landed inside one driver iteration: Ava died
organically (→Petra) and the forced fifth kill then fired on
Petra (→Catalina). The driver's per-iteration chain detector
compares bearer <redacted> before vs after the whole iteration, so it
recorded ONE entry — Ava→Catalina, tagged scriptedFifth=true,
with Ava's correct organic cause ("the wild") — and the
Ava→Petra handoff appears nowhere in chainDeaths. The game
records stay correct: fifthDeath is Petra→Catalina with lin+mo
true, relPreDeath5 is petra_novak, relPost5 is catalina_reyes.
What is wrong is only the driver's collapsed list, and it
propagates: tele deaths.bearer on 28152 is 8 (1 + 7 chain
entries) instead of 9, and the naming scorer's recentDead list
inherits the collapse (Petra day-71 is absent from it — moot
for week-1 scoring, but the same list would misstate any
late-window grief). Consequence for future panels: on any seed
with a same-iteration double handoff, chainDeaths undercounts
by one and its last entry's successor name is wrong for the
first of the pair (dead person + cause right). DEATH-LEDGER as
defined (recorded entries cause-known) still passes; the caveat
is that "recorded" is doing work here. Fixing the detector to
record mid-iteration handoffs is a tackle/instrument job, not
attempted here (instrument-only run, and the forced-chain
records themselves are correct).
