## SILENCE-GEN: no silence seed — and a measurement subtlety

No fresh seed repeats 26155's silence. But 27154 shows why
first-bearer naming can UNDERSTATE grief: its d+7 pair (Denise,
Sreyashi) never names Kavya — they grieve JORGE, the second bearer,
who died on expedition before day 25. Denise's mourning lines are
real grief ("Jorge is gone and the world just... continues"), and
Sreyashi resumes Jorge's unfinished thread ("You're wearing Jorge's
shoes now"). When a second death lands inside week 1, the village
grieves the newer death. The scorer measures first-bearer naming by
design; the sense-read catches the rest. Future naming panels on
bands with fast organic chains should report "grieves any recent
dead" alongside "names the first dead," or they will misread a
grieving village as a silent one.
