## 3. The build (plain words)

When the village is blocked on the wave-5 armed count while holding
a pair of veterans, a living villager says so at the morning briefing,
in plain words — who blooded the beasts, how many hands carry a
weapon, how many the fifth draft waits for. If a scholar died in the
wild since the last time anyone said it, the same breath names the
dead scholars and counts them. Nothing else changes: no gate, price,
deed, or demand moves.

Example lines (from the proof test, seed 99):

> Ellie: "Ivan and Dylan blooded the fourth draft's beasts — we all saw
> it on the cameras. But only 4 of us carry a weapon, and the fifth
> draft waits until 6 of us do. We need more hands armed, not more
> speeches."
>
> Yuki, quieter: "We buried Kebede and Boris out there — 2 scholars dead
> in the wild while the fifth draft waits. We keep sending our reader
> out alone."

Mechanics, exact:

- `src/js/standing.js` — new `_standingShortfallVoice()`: fires only
  when `standingDeferral(5)` is deferred AND veterans ≥ 2 (pair held)
  AND armed < demand (6). Speaker: a living non-scholar roster member,
  preferring someone who is not one of the veterans being named
  (perception-first: they live with the armed count and saw the
  veteran kills on camera; the village knows its own blooded fighters
  by first name — same justification as `_standingMournFallen`'s
  true-name use). The wave-5 TITLE is never named (knowledge gate:
  numbered drafts only, matching the hold beat). Cadence: first firing
  + every 7 days, mirroring the hold beat. The speaker pick uses a
  dedicated mulberry32 stream seeded from (day, veteran ids) — the
  voice never consumes the main Math.random stream (companionKillBeat
  convention). Churn line: reads `v.scholarDeaths`, wild causes only
  ('the wild', 'the expedition'); first firing looks back 7 days, later
  firings name only deaths since the last voicing, so no death is
  named twice. Returns true when it fired; never throws.
- `src/js/game.js` — `villageBriefing()` calls it (guarded try/catch)
  after `flowVillageKnowledge()`. The briefing already gates on
  `systemArrived`.
- `src/js/ledger.js` — `playerDeath()` writes the scholar-only
  memorial `v.scholarDeaths[]` ({day, cause, name}) next to the
  existing `v.fallen` push. `v.fallen` mixes scholar and villager
  deaths, so it cannot tell this story. Additive record only — no read
  of it changes any death, succession, or grief path.
- `docs/ONTOLOGY.md` — regenerated by `validate-ontology.js` (provides
  + `standing_no_silent_gates` rule text).
