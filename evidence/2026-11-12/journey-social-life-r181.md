  wild" and three to monsters. The pellet-bearer mantle passed seven

  its center to the next person and losing them anyway. Only 47
  conversations before the end; the social fabric never got a chance.
- **43703 (62 days):** the strongest of the four at the end — 8 alive,
  led in practice by Bridget the HVAC tech (trust 88, 5 abilities)
  and Rosa the line cook (trust 90). Boris the blacksmith stayed warm
  and steady. Nine dead, six to the wild and one to Big-Eyes. The
  vendetta pair of the panel lives here: Nina and Sam armed plots
  against each other six times in nineteen days — a real grudge,
  prosecuted in public, with nobody able to settle it.
- **43704 (111 days):** the longest run, and the most social — 128
  conversations, 22 feasts, 3 moots. Six alive at the end: Jerome the
  rail conductor (now the scholar), Omar the piano tuner (trust 90),
  Adaeze the teacher, Raj the pharmacist, Wanting the paramedic. The
  mantle passed eight times. Seventeen dead, ten to the wild. The
  village talked its way through 111 days and still lost to the
  same thing that kills every village: the wild, not each other.

What is missing, across all four: nobody survives long enough for
the social systems to compound. Bonds form (30 accepted this panel),
plots arm and spring (11 plots, 10 springs), feasts happen — but the
villages die at day 48–111, so the friendships never get a second
season. That is the standing survival fact, not a social defect.
