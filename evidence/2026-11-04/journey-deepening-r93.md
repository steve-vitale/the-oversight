## Village assessment, in plain words

Three villages made it through the deepening window and one did
not — but the story this run is about what two different numbers
mean. A village can be "regional" because it has kept one
friendship alive for three weeks, and still own zero standing
because standing counts kills, feasts, and realms, not
friendships. One village proved it twice over: regional on day
47 with nothing to its name, then standing built kill by kill
afterwards. Another never made a single friend and still climbed
to 125 standing on its own fighting and feasting. Nobody here is
broken; the two dials just measure different things, and a player
watching only one of them will misread their village.
