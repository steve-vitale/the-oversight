# Relay office lifetime: a dead person cannot block the post — 2026-10-15

This closes the concrete dependency found while authoring the Voice's fitting
deed. The predecessor's death left switchboard.holderId occupied, so a later
person could not claim or receive the role. The office's current-person deed
was correct to refuse that borrowed work; the post needed to become vacant.

## Runtime repair

comms.js settles the office after actual registerDeath, after a successful
load, and at the communications tick for old in-memory states. It reads the
canonical deathSeen book (including day zero), with memorial/dead-flag support
for older saves, then calls the existing removeSwitchboard path. The network
stays live and the ordinary appointment beat returns. No successor is silently
appointed. Living holders, nonfatal injuries and successful death refusals keep
the post. Read-only office/availability queries remain pure. Original death
and load return values are preserved; errors are reported.

No office cost, food rule, God build/day gate, enemy stat, reward or Table rule
was changed. Existing role costs become payable again when the ordinary policy
chooses another holder. That change in opportunity can affect a whole campaign.
This experiment isolates it from the earlier class-age-to-office deed change.

## Decision and follow-up

Retain correct death/vacancy behavior and explicit responsibility. Restoring a
corpse's permanent post, undoing the fitting deed, cutting costs or buffing food
to recover a prior win rate would conceal the defect rather than explain the
tradeoffs. **Balance is not closed:** the maker community signal and other
warnings remain open and visible in ROADMAP.md and SURVIVAL-LEVERS.md.

A separate existing clock mismatch is now reproduced: relayTick is described
as -1 grounding/day and advances holderDays, but the commsTick wrapper invokes
it every advancePart with no daily guard. Four real commsTick calls on the
same day 85 reduce grounding 100 -> 99 -> 98 -> 97 -> 96. The standalone shared-
harness clock probe and raw output are retained. This does not prove it caused
all six losses; it is an independent price/timing inconsistency to fix and
measure **before** deciding whether the policy or tuned price needs changing.
The lifetime fix did not introduce that clock, but newly reclaimed offices
make its costs matter again.

After settling that clock, investigate the bot's automatic vacancy filling and optional Relay work
against public goals, build, food and grounding costs. Some reclaims have large
stocks, while some repeated appointments happen under pressure. Use a separately
named policy experiment on the same runtime and independent holdout seeds;
do not present a changed-policy comparison as a game-only lever. The current
game-only comparator correctly rejects mismatched policy/harness revisions.
Keep Table access, community, survival, resources, classes and earned gear
visible. No replacement target win rate is adopted. This is one dependency
closed in the larger roadmap, not completion of all god deeds or campaigns.
