## Village assessment (plain words)

**The lead road is a coronation march; the belong road is a gauntlet.** All four leadseat villages reached national (d78, remarkably consistent) and won at the table (d89–131). Only one of four belongseat villages reached national (d73, won d89); the other three died at d60–75 — all to COMBAT (monster fights), not tribute starvation (2/1/0 starvation deaths). Same seeds, same monsters: the lead villages win the fights the belong villages die in. The belong road pays ~5,000 kcal/week OUT in tribute while courting, but the deaths are combat, not hunger — the subordinate's fighters are thinner (less food → worse condition) or the policy courts when it should be drilling. This is a road-winnability finding for the sim loops (needs 8–16 seeds to confirm); not fixed here.

**Who lived:** the lead villages ended with 8–11 alive, led by their speakers (Zuri, Hugo, Amina...), knowledge breadth high, integration stage 3, waves 4–5. **Who died:** bearers cycled (combat), villagers fell in wave 3–4 fights. **What was missing:** the realm in the villagers' mouths after the echo (the blank, fixed); a longer national season (see pacing note).

**1616-belongseat** is the poignant one: invited to the table at REGIONAL scale (lesser seat needs only ≥ regional — by design, TABLE.md), then village-lost at d60 before answering. The invitation arrived; the village couldn't survive to take the seat. Working as designed, and human.
