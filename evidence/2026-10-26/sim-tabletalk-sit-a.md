## PICK-TIME vs DAILY PANTRY (a measurement refinement, stated, not hidden)

The tableline arm's situation table used policy-daily snapshots;
this arm's snapshots are taken inside the haven tick, at the fire.
The two disagree intraday on several fires, because the pantry
moves between the policy-daily moment and the haven-tick moment
(meals, deposits, spoilage — `pantryKcalLive` itself is a pure sum
and cannot cause this). Examples, same day, both real:
- Day 114 T0: pick-time 0 (empty) vs daily 2,510 (ok-class by the
  1000 threshold — the class itself flips).
- Day 154 T0: pick-time 8,330 vs daily 5,037 (both ok, different
  numbers).
- Day 107 T3: pick-time 11,701 vs daily 10,381.
- Day 122 T3: pick-time 0 vs daily 486 (empty vs low — class flips).
- Day 75 T0: both 10,305 (agree).
The judged values in this arm are the pick-time ones: they are what
a situation-aware picker would read. Any future arm that compares
pantry numbers across arms must say which moment it means.
