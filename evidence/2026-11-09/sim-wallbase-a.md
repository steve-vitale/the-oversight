## What the numbers say

The wall stands, with a new shape. Under competent play on fresh
seeds: **national 0/16, wins 0/16, entered 0/16** — same as every
competent-only wall read before it. But two things moved relative to
the October wall reads:

1. **The table is offered almost everywhere now (14/16 invited)** and
   wave 5 is reached (2/16) — the reachability ladder below national
   is no longer the binding constraint. Competent never sits (it
   never answers the Table by design), so entered/wins stay 0 by
   policy, not by game gating. The national bar is the wall: standing
   peaks at 365 (seed 110008, died day 125) and villages sit at
   regional rank 1 with standing 150–365 and never convert.
2. **No polity forms at all (maxFires 0/16).** Not one seed builds a
   second fire, let alone a four-fire realm — under competent play,
   villages never link outward. This is the scale-onramp story in one
   number: the climb to national runs through links and polities, and
   competent villages never start climbing. (Loop B's trust-growth +
   polity probe, in flight on scale-onramp, is the live investigation
   of exactly this.)

Seed 110002 is the closest thing to a success: survives the full 150
days, reaches wave 5, Arc 4, standing 325, table invited — and still
ends at regional rank 1, no national, no seat. Seed 110004 reaches
wave 5 and dies day 138 at standing 275. Wave 5 is reachable; the
national conversion is not, on this policy.

## Sense (SENSE-TESTING LAW)

Sampled beats from seeds 110002 (wave-5 survivor), 110008 (highest
standing, 365), 110003 (died day 57). Villagers act like people in
the small: they eat by the fire, re-pack bundles checking each thing
twice, patch seams knot after careful knot, sweep the hall, set out
to find friends ("Won't be long. The village needs friends beyond the
fire."), head out for water ("Thirst doesn't negotiate. Going."),
teach the kids snares instead of foraging when the pantry is full
("Plenty for days. Teaching the kids snares instead — they'll need to
know."), linger over the fire together. The daily texture is coherent
and human.

Two honest blemishes, both in the ambient layer, neither a blank this
run fills:

- **Template repetition:** the same ambient line repeats verbatim for
  the same villager on the same day (seed 110002 day 5: "patching a
  seam with a scrap of cloth, knot after careful knot" twice for
  gen_uumuam1; "eating by the fire — not talking, just eating" is the
  default eat line for most villagers most days). The chores read as
  a small deck of lines, not as a lived day.
- **Say-beats dwarf ambient beats** (~3,000–3,700 say lines vs
  60–162 ambient per seed): the transcript is mostly System
  narration, not villager voice. Nothing robotic or contradictory was
  found in the sampled lines; the village feels alive at the fire
  and quiet everywhere else.

## Village assessment in plain words

Sixteen fresh villages, played the careful, competent way — no risks,
no heroics, nobody answering the Table's call. They live a good while
(typical village 119 days, three survive the full 150), they climb to
wave 4 every time and two even reach wave 5, and the Table invites
fourteen of them to sit. But none of them ever becomes a nation, none
sits at the Table, none wins. They die the way villages die here: in
fights with monsters, out on expeditions and in the village itself —
four out of five deaths are monster combat. Almost nobody starves.
And none of them ever builds a second fire or links to another village
— they live and die as one hearth, however strong they get. The wall
isn't food and it isn't reaching the high waves anymore; it's that a
careful village never becomes a bigger thing. The road from a strong
village to a nation runs through other villages, and these villages
never take it.
