Does giving winseek a sit drive turn the 3 offered-not-taken seeds of the frozen band into sits, without buying starvation or survival? ## The shape decision (from the code read — recorded here, not re-decided later)

**Port `takeSeatDrive` into winseek as a new composite policy file — do NOT lift it into base competent.** Reasons: (1) `competent.js` is frozen substrate shared by every competent-derived policy (doorwalk, linkfeast, stageally, multisweep, …) — a drive there changes every baseline those arms measure against, and a default-OFF gate in the base still puts a new branch in the hottest path in the repo. (2) The established precedent is composition, not base edits: `takeseat.js` (2026-10-17, feastscale substrate, 13/16 tables sat) and `competent-linkfeast-b.js` (2026-11-07, TAKESEAT_B=1, 4/8 lesser earned → sat) both add the drive as a layer on top of a frozen base, gated off by default, zero `src/js`. (3) The gap is a winseek property, not a competent property: doorwalk sits 6/8 on the same band without any base change; winseek is the policy with no drive. The fix belongs to the policy that lacks it.
- New file `scripts/policies/winseek-sitdrive-b.js`: `winseek` + the drive, gate `SITDRIVE_B=1` (default OFF). The drive itself is the `competent-linkfeast-b.js` shape, copied (classified skips, module-level STATS, at-Haven check, `tsSeatTaken` + `pg.tableDone` never-twice) — the codebase already carries this drive twice (`takeseat.js` vs linkfeast-b, "copied verbatim"), and a third copy in the policy that needs it follows that precedent rather than coupling winseek's measurement to linkfeast's module. No change to `winseek.js`, `competent.js`, or any `src/js`.
- Gating preserved, all of it (the engine owns the day-90 floor — never bypassed): never while `Game.over`; never while a `tbfight` is live (classified skip `fight`); never twice (`ctx.tsSeatTaken` + `pg.tableDone`); requires `pg.tableAnnounced`; requires the scholar at Haven (`playerAtHaven` — classified skip `away`); reads `tablePreview()` — null is a classified skip (`preview-null`: not staged / not earned / pre-day-90, the engine's answer); not-ready is a classified skip carrying the engine's why (`not-ready:<why>`); only a ready preview calls the real `Game.tableTakeSeat()`, and the result (ok/fail, day, seat kind, doors offered via `scholar.tableChoices`) is recorded. The drive never answers doors (`chooseTableOption` is winseek's pre-existing `tableRoad`, which drains first-option beats after a sit exactly as it would for a human player who sat — that behavior is unchanged by this arm and is NOT re-decided here), never picks a sitter (engine default: the player character), never manufactures an invitation.
- The drive runs in `upkeep` (after `winseek.upkeep`: competent upkeep + hunt patrol + contest road), exactly as linkfeast-b runs it after its base upkeep — so it fires up to 3×/day and catches the first ready day. The sitstep snapshot runs once/day in `daily()`, so the cumulative `takeseatStats` counters remain the only per-day attribution channel (same as sitgap-b).

## GO-bar arithmetic — the correction (checked BEFORE this prereg was committed)

The foundation arm's brief carried a `sits ≥5/8` bar. That bar is **arithmetically impossible for a sit-drive-only change and is corrected here before any computation**: only 3/8 seeds ever reach day 90 alive on the frozen band under winseek (53001 d119, 53002 d122, 53007 d96; the other five die d59–d80, median survival 75d). A sit drive can only sit seeds that live to see a ready preview — at most 3/8. The 5/8 bar would have judged the drive against deaths it cannot touch. **The honest bar is: all reachable seeds sit (3/3) and the offered-not-taken class is empty.** The day-90-floor survival half (5/8 dying at a 75d median) is a separate follow-up, NOT this arm — stated in the brief and restated here so the verdict cannot be re-read as a survival claim.
