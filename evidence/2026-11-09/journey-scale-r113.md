## What this means for winnable

The lesser seat works (4/4 priced). The full seat does not, and r113 names
the walls more precisely than r112 did. (1) The propose wall: on half the
seeds, trade is never proposed because no unlinked village ever reaches the
opinion-10 bar — the climb (one codex gift + broadcast wins) is too slow for
the villages the courtship actually meets. (2) The seasoning wall behind it:
where trade links do form, they season for 2–3 days and then flicker below
40 — leader-death crises hit every trade link −10 at once, concede returns
only +8, and the links gain ~2 trust in 25 days. The next levers, in order:
tend peer links (the r92 TEND_PEER arm, never wired into this policy) to
hold trust above the bar, and a faster opinion climb so the courtship knocks
earlier. (3) The veteran wall has moved: villagers kill wave-4 beasts now
(7 kills), but the tribute spends the veterans it pays — the village pays its
only two living veterans and holds none. Whether the tribute should consume
its veterans is a design question, not a bug. The feast bundle (embassy)
never formed on any seed — still the untested third path.
