# ADDENDUM to sim-titanroute-a prereg (written BEFORE any arm run)

## Correction to the screening rule and to GO (1)

The prereg as first committed said: fresh pilot seeds = first 6
baseline runs that hear the lair line; GO (1) fails if ANY non-97301
baseline shares. Screening (baseline-only data, no arm outcome exists
yet) has already shown that rule to be unworkable as written, for a
reason the coordinator's own bar anticipates: the bar reads "the
routing policy reaches tile-share on ≥6/8 seeds WHERE THE BASELINE
POLICY REACHES 0". The pilot is therefore conditioned on
baseline-zero seeds by the bar itself, and the first-committed
screening rule omitted that condition.

Screening evidence so far (baseline runs only):
- 97325: located day 76, shares days 93–99 AT HOME (4,4), 2 attempts
  day 98, both REFUSED/MUSTER-GATE.
- 97328: located day 56, shares days 59–61, 70, 81 AT HOME (4,4),
  9 attempts day 81, all REFUSED/MUSTER-GATE.
Both are the known raid-pin/lingering class (herald pinned to home,
scholar home) — the same accident as 97301, not deliberate routing.
Baseline shares are common once located (3 of the 5 located fresh
seeds so far share this way). A pilot that included them and then
failed GO (1) "because baseline shares" would condemn the routing
policy for accidents the baseline already has; that is not what the
coordinator's bar measures.

CORRECTED RULE (applies from this addendum forward; no arm run has
started, so no arm outcome can have influenced it):
- Fresh pilot seeds = the first 6 seeds in numeric order from 97310
  satisfying BOTH (a) baseline hears the lair line AND (b) baseline
  shareCount = 0.
- Excluded-by-(b) seeds (97325, 97328) stay in the screening record
  and in the evidence file as baseline raid-share evidence — they
  are not hidden, they are not pilot seeds.
- GO (1) (corrected, matching the coordinator's bar): arm reaches
  tile-share on ≥6/8 pilot seeds, where the pilot seeds are, by
  construction, baseline-zero except disclosed 97301 (whose
  raid-pin share is the known exception, reported separately and
  never pooled as a routing success for either arm). In other
  words: the 6 fresh + 97307 are baseline-zero by construction;
  the bar is arm ≥6/8 total.
- GO (2) and guardrail (3) are UNCHANGED.
- This correction is NOT a softening: the original gloss would have
  made GO (1) fail automatically on screening data alone, before
  the routing policy ever ran. The corrected bar is the
  coordinator's bar as written. If the corrected bar is itself
  judged wrong, that judgement belongs under "metric review" for
  the NEXT run, not in this run's verdict.
