But the audience weeps, and the galaxy learns that the feared one chose gentleness — and that choice echoes longer than any threat. The terror stays, pledged now: a shield over every village, forever.
You learned to read the ground and to leave some of its answers where they grew. Another hungry person would come this way.
You remembered the weather, the small signs, the water found before it was too late. You passed the directions on.
◈ THE ARCHITECT — Boris Kowalski ascended through doors you built: relay. The galaxy will remember the god's name. The village will remember who built the door. Both are true. Neither is small.
The terms held — the table had priced the village, ascension and all, and the village paid in full.
◈ THE OVERSIGHT —
You won. Say it plainly: you won. The village endures. The table signed. The galaxy applauds, and the applause is real, and you earned it.
And somewhere out there — past the cameras, past the light — in rows of humming glass, the little ones are still napping.
The mourners asked you, and you changed the subject.
The System put a pod on the screen, and you let the segment play.
They are still there. Still small. Still waiting for someone to decide they matter more than the moment.
That is what the word means. Not the watching — anyone can watch. The oversight is the looking away. The most terrible thing the System ever did, it did by accident, because it was bored. You did it on purpose, because you were busy.
The village will tell your story for years. It will be a good story. Listen for the part they don't tell.
