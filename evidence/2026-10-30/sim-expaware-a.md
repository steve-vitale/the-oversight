## Plain words first

Every monster meeting in the game comes through one of a handful
of doors, and only one door lets you see the beast in time. On
32 seeds and 4,379 meetings: wild field meetings (2,360) and raid
meetings (1,174) never evade — their pre-contact outcome is the
beast scattering (73% at wave 1, collapsing to 0% for wild wave-4
meetings). Expedition meetings (571) are the only ones that evade
(327 of them, 57%) and the only ones that never scatter. Contest
fights (166) and psycho fights (5) never do anything pre-contact
at all — sealed arenas and ambushes go straight to blows, every
time. At wave 4 the escape hatches are nearly gone: of 100
wave-4 meetings, 94 are fights, 3 are scatters, 3 are evades,
and 0 are kills. A wave-4 meeting is a fight you mostly flee
(64) or die in (29).

"Warned" at wave 4 means the village has met this beast type
before (65 of 100 meetings) or knows its counter (38 of 100).
Being warned does not save you: warned villagers flee 65% of the
time and die 29% of the time; unwarned villagers flee 63% and die
29% — the same fight, the same ending. The one place warning
changes anything is the Eulogy, whose counter is not a weapon but
a trick: villagers who know it stop striking and contradict the
beast's narration instead ("it says Jovan runs — Jovan
advances"), dealing exactly zero damage by design. That is why
warned wave-4 damage looks lower on average — it is one beast's
trick, not a warned effect: outside the Eulogy, warned and
unwarned damage are the same (means 25.3 vs 27.5). Scout
sightings, rumors, and heard shouts before a meeting are
UNMEASURED, not absent: no recorder and no engine state records
who heard what before contact, and this arm refuses to fake one
from a global "someone shouted somewhere" feed — that would
break the perception law it exists to uphold.

## Sense-testing law (all 35 wave-4 first-meeting transcripts read)

S1 — ALL 35 first meetings are unwarned, and all 35 read
unwarned. This is structural for W1 (a first meeting per
seed+mid cannot have a prior same-type meeting — same mid
means same wave, so no earlier meeting with that type exists
anywhere in the seed) and empirical for W2 (no first meeting
carries a known counter: 0/35 trickKnown). The sense leg's
warned side therefore cannot be judged from first meetings
alone — disclosed here, not hidden — and the warned side was
judged from a 7-transcript warned-repeat sample (post-hoc
extension, see S4). On the unwarned side the leg passes
cleanly: no first-meeting transcript shows recognition, a
counter, or prior-knowledge language. The villager just
fights, flees, dies, or (on the expedition door) sees it in
time or walks straight into it.

S2 — the two failed-awareness lines are the most honest lines
in-band: "Jorge walks straight into The Eater. No avoiding
it." / "Lauren walks straight into The Congregation. No
avoiding it." The awareness check ran (expedition door), it
failed, and the consequence is immediate and nearly lethal
(85/90 taken in round one, 1-round flees). The evade lines
are their mirror: "saw The Algorithm in time and gave it
room" / "saw Big-Eyes in time and gave it room" (the strike,
unknown — generic phrase, knowledge-gating holds). The
expedition door at wave 4 reads exactly as the wiring says:
one check, two outcomes, no middle.

S3 — the repeated "has Bow/Crossbow/Sling but no arrows — the
string stays drawn, useless" line recurs across first meetings
(Sigrun, Mateo, Guilherme, Shuang, Lauren, Xoan, and more).
It is not a narration bug — it is the engine honestly
reporting fighters entering wave-4 fights with ranged weapons
and no ammunition — but its frequency is a sense finding in
its own right: wave-4 fighters repeatedly arrive unable to use
the weapon they carry. It echoes the ammo lever's standing
gap (pocketed ammunition exists; quivers/arrow supply at
wave-4 readiness does not reliably follow). Reported, not
chased (DIAG arm).

S4 — warned-repeat sample (post-hoc, 7 transcripts): the
Eulogy trick lines are the warned narration working as
designed — "contradicts the narration" + "holds the line —
working the trick, not the damage race" appear ONLY in
trick-known fights (21/21) and never in trick-unknown fights
(0/9). A warned Eulogy meeting reads warned: the villager
fights differently because they know the counter. Non-Eulogy
warned repeats (e.g., reunion FLEE, "Your Move hits for 69,
strikes for 30, runs") read exactly like unwarned fights —
no recognition language, no counter narration, just blows and
flight. That matches the numbers: warning changes the Eulogy
fight's shape and nothing else's. The one warned-side narration
gap: no wave-4 fight transcript, warned or not, ever says the
villager recognizes the beast ("we've seen this") — the log
is blows, gear, and trick lines only. Whether recognition
should be narrated is a design question for the backlog, not
a bug claim: the engine never promised it.

S5 — within-fight shouts are narrated, pre-contact shouts are
not (and cannot be, from this corpus): "is losing — shouts for
help! Elena charges in!" / "Elena answered Guilherme's shout,
but Guilherme is past holding" appear in first-meeting
transcripts. These are DURING-fight shouts with a named
answerer — the shoutrec signal. They are not evidence for or
against a pre-contact heard shout: nobody's pre-meeting
hearing is recorded anywhere, which is why SHOUT stays
UNMEASURED rather than being retrofitted from these lines.
