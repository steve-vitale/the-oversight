## Rule and caller boundaries

An actual known monster must fall while the hunter survives. Its fresh
opening body HP must strictly exceed the hunter's opening full-body maximum,
not their current wounds. Stalker can hunt solo with their own preparation,
food, equipment and magic. Beastmaster can use support if they held Pack Hunt
at opening, actually paid/called it on that prey, and every allied fighter
remains alive and present. Holding the talent or borrowing somebody else's
animals is insufficient. Pack Hunt is an active root/harry effect, not an
extra animal fighter. Its existing70kcal price remains.

The tactical constructor records prey, people, full-body capacity and held
Pack Hunt. Turn/damage/outcome callers retain allied participation and losses;
paid Pack Hunt marks its actual caster and prey. Snake segments form one
lineage and must all fall. A routed live animal is not a kill. Door re-entry
restores prior wounds after the constructor: each prey is checked again
before its first hit so that a regenerated opening template cannot erase
those wounds. Later damage during this hunt does not revoke eligibility.

Field fights use the actual fresh HP draw, not the template's random upper
bound. Persisted bodies need a known equal HP/maximum; unknown prior wounds
earn no receipt. Only the slain lead qualifies: field settlement may mark
other packmates gone when they scattered. Available support precludes solo
credit even if its body never joins; the pack route requires actual paid use
and surviving help. Lifeline's real prevention of an ally's fall remains
valid. The known distant companion30HP proxy cannot cheapen the threshold;
NPC capacity has the field engine's100HP floor.

The first receipt belongs to the canonical person and survives saving,
loading and that same person's later succession into the player role.
A different successor gets no inheritance. No old accomplishments are
invented; an old mid-fight save without opening evidence cannot earn credit.
Existing active gods remain. Class, payoff, four path abilities and day80
remain required. The Table still never requires a god. Telemetry reuses the
trigger's timestamp without consuming a clock or random draw.

Scope limit: tactical credit is for actors captured in the constructor's
opening ledger. Late/formal-only injected NPC casters do not acquire their
own opening evidence here; they still count as support against solo credit
when observed. Their entry timing and the previously documented formal
companion body/gear parity need follow-up. This unit does not promise every
late joiner a deed, complete titan/contest parity, or resolve the Garden and
reactor questions.
