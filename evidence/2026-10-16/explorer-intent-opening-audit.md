no Game, shared loader, default controller, price, XP, kit, region or nomad
change. The eight authored deeds/two remaining proxies are unchanged.

Each named controller answers its own actual introductory knack, through
`liveKnackChoices` and `chooseKnack`, using only shown options. Control then
follows capable. Build adds shown Explorer offer preference and replacement

actual travel/forage when necessary. The shared loader leaves idle policies
unanswered. This preserves the earlier knack-policy audit's ownership rule.

preferences within a mixed capable strategy, not certified pacifist policies
or proof of two complete, distinct playstyles. No full kit is installed.

or three unsuccessful daily attempts. It requires camp, at least60HP and
1200kcal, at most320 used ticks and no live fight, encounter, contest or
pending choice. Extra conversations require Haven and actual present known
residents. These budgets are Agent-authored policy levers, not Game balance.
