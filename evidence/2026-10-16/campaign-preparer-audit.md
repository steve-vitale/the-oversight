The final `campaigner-preparer` inherits campaigner-rations and adds:

- One extra nut-shelling batch per current person/day, using real Shell in
  the pack first, or the counter if that budget remains. Shelling pays4 ticks,
  preserves units/source metadata and produces the real75-percent net food.
- At a usable camp, Stage unprocessed before sorting an opaque carried bag.
  Sort at most one extra counter bag, paying8 ticks. Choose a helper by their
  public general plant knowledge, not the bag's hidden species. Existing base
  work/sorting still runs; this is a bound on the added helper, not all work.
- Use native bulk Put away finished food only with safe finished neighbors,
  actual pantry access and space. Do not move one chosen batch through a
  private API, waive exile, or invent contribution credit. Dangerous finished
  neighbors can prevent the safe batch from being shelved too.
- Keep1800 banked kcal,50HP and the dayTicks<=384 work window; stop after a
  new encounter, contest, day, death/succession or ending. No bonded or spoiled
  nut input. Repeated hooks share the same person/day budget.

Physical processing, storage, contribution and recruitment are distinct.
`campaignPrepKcal` counts newly edible kcal from an actual shell transform;
`campaignPrepStoredKcal` counts actual pantry growth. The existing shelving
verb adds no `gives` credit. No controller writes any such ledger or grants
skills, knowledge, food, health, travel, time, slots or campaign gates.
