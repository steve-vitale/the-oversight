## Outcome and survival levers

Undying Ward now requires carrying a real hostile wound intended for another
known living person, then both leaving that fight alive. Won, routed,
calmed and genuine permitted withdrawal qualify. No kill or arbitrary wound
quota is required. A paid tap, a fully absorbed hit, friendly damage, an
anonymous helper, a dead participant or a losing result does not qualify.
The first personal receipt is immutable. Pending tactical evidence survives
save/load; old saves without it get no invented accomplishment. Existing
active gods remain. Day80, complete class, payoff and four-path kit stay.

The separate Aegis prerequisite fixes two actual routes: NPC planners/casts
can select the bearer, and the hit is composed once for the real protector.
The old target's armor and phantom damage history no longer apply first;
the protector's armor and source rally apply once. Expired or departed links
stop. Nested options preserve finalized beam context. This can raise a
protector's actual wound compared with the old double-armor route, while
opening reciprocal protection. It does **not** establish recalculation of
beam resistance for a different target. Food prices and damage values stay.

Real tactical Aegis/Leech and named field Aegis/Bodyguard observe outcomes.
The field observer reuses the existing random draw and attributes Aegis only
when its existing diversion actually displaced the ally's hit. Draw Fire's
override cannot manufacture an Aegis receipt. Leech's existing split/costs
stay. Field helper70HP/gear and formal NPC30HP/gear models remain separate
known limitations, as does broader NPC Leech availability. Ward's god-scale
intervention, audience and strategic promises remain in SCALE-AUDIT.
