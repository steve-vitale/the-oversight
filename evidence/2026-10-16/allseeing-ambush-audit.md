follows them into the mantle. New player payments also name their owner,
so a successor cannot borrow a predecessor's paid day. Old player saves'

that terminal result neither pays nor emits another event. A settled ordinary

unfunded holders and targets outside the living resident boundary retain the
ordinary process. This is protection from the invitation's deception, not
immunity to a direct assault. Player foiling still adds its actual Codex doubt;
NPC foiling does not manufacture evidence in the bearer's Codex. Telemetry
names the actual target, and the public line says that person declined.
