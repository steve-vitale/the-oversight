# Growth-line stale promise — investigation memo (r245, Lane A leg 2 deepening)

- Date: 2026-11-19. Investigation only — no src changed, no panel, no gate.
- Question: r244 found the dawn growth line promising blades the morning could not deliver (seed 105024, day 86). Is that a game defect, what exactly moves between the voice and the call, and what game-side fix closes it?
- Verdict: **game defect, confirmed and mechanism pinned.** The line is a promise about a future act, spoken at dawn, with no re-check when the act happens — and the world keeps moving in between. For a real player the gap is worse than in the harness: they may hear the line at dawn and tap "Sound the call" hours later, after fights, contests, travel and deaths. A driver-only gate would hide the lie, not fix it (r244's verdict stands).

## Plain words: what the player experiences

At dawn the game says, in the log the player reads on waking: "The fires have warmed since the call — The Hungry Road would send 2 more blades if the call were sounded again. The muster would stand at 11 of the 10 THE HERALD demands — enough. Sound the call." The player sounds the call. Nobody new answers. The muster stands at 8, not 11. The game told the player to do a thing, the player did it, and nothing happened. The advice was wrong, and the game never says why.

## The numbers (seed 105024, r244 evidence)

- Dawn day 86, the line promised: 2 allied blades, muster 9 → 11 of 10, "enough".
- First upkeep day 86 (growthWatch): muster 8, harvestable 0, Haven blades 3 → 2, Haven volunteers 1 → 0. The call that morning (musterLog day 86) harvested 0.
- Day 87 the same machinery worked exactly: the line promised 1 Haven volunteer, the commit brought exactly 1 (Haven 2 → 3). The defect is intermittent, not constant — which is why it reads as a lie rather than a broken feature.
- One Haven villager died that morning: `gen_uiy4q4k`, cause "contest", day 86 (telemetry deaths; village pop 7 → 6 between the day-85 and day-86 standing traces). The other 6 heard lines panel-wide were exact.

## Exact ordering: what runs between the voice and the call

The voice and the call read the same pure projection (`titanMusterGrowth`, src/js/titans.js:677) but at different moments, with live game code in between:

1. `Game.sleep()` (src/js/game.js:21726) ticks the night in 32-tick chunks. `tickAction` (game.js, ~20678) fires `endDay()` (game.js:34038) mid-chunk when the day's tick budget is spent. (Proved for this night: the pacing sample before sleep on day 85 shows dayTicks 344 — the day had not already rolled during the waits.)
2. Inside `endDay`, in order: `titanDawnTick()` (game.js:34454 → titans.js:240ish: omens, escalation, `titanMusterUpkeep` titans.js:1361, `titanTurnTick` titans.js:1310) runs *before* the line; `scholar.day += 1` (game.js:34412); dawn header; `attemptWaitingOutings()` (game.js:34499); then, last beat, `titanMusterGrowthLine(scholar.day)` (game.js:34505 → titans.js:1426), which calls `titanMusterGrowth` and speaks the promise via `Game.say` — the dawn log the player reads. Then `save()`. The r236 fix put the line here deliberately, after the outings, so it names the pool a call sounded that morning would find. The pool it names is the pool *at that instant*.
3. Sleep's tail (game.js, ~21890–21925: healing, energy, nightmares, `sickRestTick`) touches only the scholar. No pool moves here.
4. Back in the harness day loop (scripts/sim-harness.js:589–591): `Game.sleep()` returns, then `driveContests` runs (`driveContestsImpl`, sim-harness.js:785: while a contest is active, `contestChoose(0)` and fights). Contest deaths are real deaths (`_contestDie` / `_cxKillContestant`, src/js/contests.js header ~41–113): a villager can die on camera between the dawn line and the first upkeep. A real player has the same exposure plus everything else they do before tapping the call.
5. Next loop iteration, first upkeep (sim-harness.js:565 → driver `policy.upkeep`, scripts/journey-deepening-r244-20261119.js:981): `musterPlay` runs FIRST (r244:2169). It re-reads the projection (growthWatch, r244:2244), then `musterDecision` (r244:2128) fires the re-call on the heard line alone — `call = ... || !!s.growthHeard` — with no fresh-projection check. `soundTitanCall` (titans.js:862) asks `titanAlliedFaces` (titans.js:512) for faces; none are free, so it says "has no free fighters to send" and brings 0.

## What moved the pool — pinned, and unpinned

- **Haven blade, PINNED.** The day-86 contest death (`gen_uiy4q4k`) is stamped day 86. EndDay's own contest block runs while `scholar.day` is still 85, so a day-86 stamp puts the death *after* the day increment — after the dawn line, inside step 4 above. Haven blades 3 → 2 and volunteers 1 → 0 in the same window. A committed blade died on camera after the game promised the morning's pool.
- **Allied 2 blades, UNPINNED at the micro level.** No per-link snapshot exists in the r244 evidence (growthWatch rows carry no link detail), and my one replay probe of seed 105024 diverged — different trajectory, no growth line heard at all (r244 already warned probes are not bitwise reproducible). What can be ruled out: trust collapse (the link was trusted, quota 6, at dawn; trust cannot fall 70+ → under 25 in one morning — the biggest single moves are −8 per refused call and −2/day muster hunger); and the turn (allies still 6 at musterPlay, and `titanTurnTick` runs before the line). Remaining candidates are the two free adults dying or leaving the roster in the window — no allied death is telemetered that day, and no regional event is logged days 84–87 — or the link itself dropping out of the projection. The re-panel instrument should record the full per-link projection at line time (my probe patch did this for growthWatch; the same detail belongs in the line-time trace). The structural conclusion does not depend on which candidate it was: *any* of them can move the pool, and the line has no defence against any of them.

## The Haven clause has the same hole

r236 closed Haven at the *commit*: the driver only re-commits on fresh `havenGain > 0`, and `titanCommitOwn` (titans.js:996) recomputes its candidates fresh on each tap, so a stale Haven promise fails gracefully ("No one is free to march"). But the *line* can still over-promise Haven the same way: it names volunteers who can die in the post-dawn contest (the day-85→86 volunteer count 1 → 0 is the shape, with a named blade dying the same morning). The fix must cover both clauses, not just the allied one — and the allied clause is the worse of the two, because the call, unlike the commit, has no fresh check at all.

## Fix options (game-side, ranked)

**1. Re-validate at the act, and reword the line as a dawn report. (Recommended, smallest.)** In `soundTitanCall` (titans.js:862), recompute `titanMusterGrowth` before sounding; if the fresh harvestable is 0, do not sound — say so honestly instead ("The fires have cooled since dawn — no new blades answer. The muster stands at N."). No refusal trust cost, no empty "THE CALL GOES OUT". In `titanMusterGrowthLine` (titans.js:1426), reword the promise into a report of the dawn state ("At dawn, The Hungry Road stood ready to send 2 more blades") so the spoken line cannot be falsified by what happens after dawn; keep the numbers, keep the alert function. *Player experience:* the dawn line still wakes them to growth, and if the morning eats the growth the call tells them plainly instead of performing an empty ritual. *What could break:* callers of `titanMusterGrowth` are only the line, the two `_growthSaid` re-arms (call and commit, titans.js:922/1051), and the driver — none change meaning; the `_growthSaid` dedupe must be re-armed on a cooled call too, or the line will repeat the stale growth tomorrow. The council's readiness line (titans.js:765) is already the safe shape — computed at the decision moment via `titanCallReadiness`, which generates rosters so projection and sound agree — and stays untouched. *Proof test:* stage a muster with a trusted link and 2 free adults; fire the growth line; then remove the free adults (kill them, or drop trust under 25) before sounding the call; assert no "THE CALL GOES OUT", the cooled-fires line was said, no trust −8 charged, no new allies — plus the mirror case (growth still fresh → call proceeds as before), and a Haven case (volunteer gone between line and commit tap → first tap says "No one is free", no second-tap promise). Model on scripts/test-journey-deepening-r244-muster-20261119.js (driver-level) plus live-game staging via scripts/sim-harness.js `loadGame`.

**2. Speak numbers only at the decision moment; dawn carries a numberless nudge.** Move the counted line out of `endDay` into the muster surface the player reads when acting (war council / `titanSelfActions`, titans.js:1075, computed fresh via `titanCallReadiness`), and leave dawn with "something has warmed — check the muster" (no numbers, no "sound the call"). *Player experience:* numbers are never older than the moment they are read. *Cost:* the line loses its alert function — a player who never opens the muster never learns growth happened (the r235 27-day silent stall returns in that case) — and it still does not fully close the hole unless combined with option 1, because a player can read the council, do other things, and tap the call later. Bigger change (app.js self-bar wiring), weaker guarantee. Take only with option 1's re-validation.

**3. Snapshot-and-hold: reserve the promised faces at dawn.** When the line speaks, mark the projected blades reserved so contests, outings and sims cannot take them before the call. *Player experience:* the promise becomes literally true. *Cost:* the largest change and the wrong direction — it freezes living villagers to honour a UI line, fighting the game's own rule that villagers act like people and the world stays live (Steve's no-faking law, 2026-11-10). Blast radius touches contest casting, outings, distant sims and the turn. Not recommended.

Also owed as harness hardening (not the fix): gate the driver's re-call on the fresh projection (`musterDecision`: re-call on `growthHeard` only when fresh harvestable > 0). It hides the lie if shipped alone; ship it after the game fix lands, as regression cover.

## Are there other dawn voices with this shape? (Sibling check)

Grepping for numeric future-yield promises ("would send / would bring / would stand") finds only two: the growth line and the council readiness line — and the council line is safe (computed at the decision moment, same tick as the call). The other dawn voices report present state or past events, not future yields: `jointFavorDawnSignal` (game.js:8926) says a villager "is waiting" with a due day — it can go stale if they leave, but it carries no numbers and no imperative to act, so the failure mode is a wasted walk, not a false muster; `dawnSynergyResonance`, `seasonTurnBeat` and `checkWaveUnlockBeat` narrate what happened or unlocked, they do not promise what an action will bring. The growth line is the only dawn voice that names a number and tells the player to act on it. Fix it there; no sibling repair is owed.
