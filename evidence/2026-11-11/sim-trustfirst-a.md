# sim-trustfirst-a — trust + gossip first numbers (Loop A, fleshout-20261111T0815Z)

**Verdict: GO** — every pre-registered row passes (see GO table). This is a
first-numbers census under default competent play, seeds 190211–190218, 150-day
death-aware horizon. No lever, no landing claim, no confirm band spent.

**In plain words:** trust is easy to earn and hard to lose in a lived village.
Villagers start out wary of the scholar and end up mostly friendly or loyal,
because every shared chore drips +1 trust and almost nothing takes it away in
bulk — except the scholar dying, which hits everyone at once. Gossip is
constant — the fires are never quiet — but four-fifths of it is routine
chatter about who left, who came back, and who was told to do what. It does
real work on reputation (a story told secondhand moves reputation more than
twice as often as seeing something yourself), and a rumor genuinely reaches a
second, third, and twelfth villager. The other villages' trust in you, though,
never moves at all: it sits at zero the whole run, and the two lines that say
it dropped by 3 or 10 name a number that cannot move. Villagers do propose
bonds to each other (about half are accepted), and they keep raising the idea
of linking villages — but every single link idea dies unanswered. Moots happen
and reach a verdict, but since the scholar never votes, no moot ever moves
anyone's trust.

Net: **+10,631 up-points, −3,152 down-points, net +7,479** across the band.
The single biggest downward force is the scholar dying (−1,103 points over
93 succession events). Everything else that lowers trust is small and rare.

Two saturation facts:

- **Half of all trust calls change nothing.** 16,034 bumpTrust calls were
  made; only 7,726 moved the map. The rest scaled to zero: above trust 90,
  small acts (+1/+2) apply 0, and at the 100 cap nothing applies at all.
  Trust is a tank that fills up and stays full.
- **Newcomers arrive already trusting you a little.** A new villager's trust
  key appears at 10–30, not at the 10 default — the join path writes an
  opening value before the instrument's setter is fitted to the key.

What never moves trust under competent play: `tendLink` (0 calls — no plain
competent path calls it), `spreadRumor` (0), `_evTrustAll` (0 — the six
"Village trust +N" lines in the code never fire), villageTalk/villageDeal/
villageShareFood/joinVillageReal (0 each — no policy calls them), and the
moot vote (player-gated; the scholar never votes, so 0 moves).

## Gossip: what it is, what it does

Gossip seeds by action (band, 10,481; 4,154 further seeds suppressed as
same-day repeats):

- departure **4,278**, order **2,532**, return **1,603** — routine movement
  chatter: **8,413 of 10,481 (80%)**.
- deed **655**, raid **454**, talk **429**, fight **80**, feast **17**,
  death **18** (+6 named deaths), rescue **21**, give_food **56**,
  loot_corpse **26**, haven_built **21**, firstaccord **8**.
- Reputation-bearing seeds (death, theft, bully, ambush cases) are rare:
  a few dozen against eight thousand routine ones.

What gossip changes: reputation, and only reputation. Of 29,769 applyRep
calls, 13,316 carried a real change — 9,419 from gossip hops, 3,856 from
direct observation. A story told secondhand moves reputation **2.4× as
often** as seeing the thing yourself. Gossip moves no trust at all (canon
law holds: the only trust cost gossip carries is the 15% caught-lying trace,
and no caught-lying event was observed in the corpus). Reputation itself
moves a lot: **12–19 villagers per seed** end with a changed reputation,
and **680** reputation dimensions crossed the ±25/±30 talk thresholds
(start vs end). But no reputation move was observed gating anything a
villager does — rep is written constantly and read rarely in sims.

Spread is real and fast: 41,639 hops, 7,726 mutated (distortion grows per
hop; ≥2 distortion mutates 45% of the time at ×1.6), 3,650 items reached a
second hearer, max 12 hearers (a whole village). 28–45 gossip items are
alive at once on any seed. A rumor does reach a second villager — usually
it reaches the whole fire.

## The four "trust" numbers, and finding #2068

The brief asked whether the numbers that share the word "trust" in say
lines are coherent. The corpus answers it by absence plus one phantom:

- **Villager trust** (`state.village.trust[vid]`): moves constantly
  (10,246 writes), saturates high, means something. The trust-gate lines
  ("Ask someone who trusts you.", "Why should I listen to you?",
  "You haven't earned that yet.", "Not today. Sorry. (Low trust —
  reluctant.)") all name this number, and they behave: dozens in the first
  days, fading as trust climbs.
- **Village opinion** (`ov.trust`): **0 everywhere, always.** No live verb
  ever raises it (villageTalk/Deal/ShareFood/joinVillageReal: 0 calls;
  probationTick: 1,044 calls, 0 moves). Two say lines name it with a
  number — and both numbers are phantoms (below).
- **Link trust** (`link.trust`): moves on only 8 links band-wide
  (tribute-weekly 59, succession-haircut 25, aid-offer-accept 32,
  titan-event 30, aid-beg-give 11, renegotiation-silence 3; 0 unattributed).
  Two links fell to 0; three seasoned. It is a thin, real number.
- **Reputation** (`state.village.rep`): moves constantly via gossip and
  observation, but is a different thing from trust by construction.

**The phantom lines.** Two lines in the corpus print a village-opinion
number that the state never records:

> "A runner from Emberhold, dusty and direct: 'Your foragers are stripping
> our side of the northeast ground. Back off — or we'll talk with teeth.'
> (Trust -3. The commons cuts both ways.)"

> "🌙 Raid — Thornfield came in the night from the north. They carried off
> ~1696 kcal from the pantry. Nobody's dead, but the message is clear: the
> ground is theirs too, and they're done asking. (Trust -10.)"

Both write `ov.trust = Math.max(0, (ov.trust || 0) - N)` — and ov.trust is
already 0, so the write is a no-op. The line claims a drop; the number
never moves. The real state that moves is `ov.commons.tension` (+10 per
complaint, +10 per raid) — the lines name the wrong number. (Raids fired
on multiple seeds; the runner fired for Emberhold, Stonebridge, and
Thornfield.)

**Finding #2068 verdict: unanswerable from lived play.** The "Village
trust +N" lines (game.js:26983 +8, 27000 +3, 27062 +2, 27075 −3, 27190 +5,
48171 +10; betrayal.js:1107 −8) never appear in the 1,600-line say corpus
(days 1–21 per seed), and `_evTrustAll` never fires in 1,150 village-days.
The events that would print them — feeding/sharing with/turning away a
traveler, completing a river trade, winning a trial by talking, a life-debt,
a mentored party member dying — never happen under default competent play.
The brief's "#2068" could not be located in sim-findings.md or any dated
evidence file (verified in the prereg); the coherence question it poses
cannot be settled from this corpus because the thing it asks about never
occurs. What the corpus does settle: the two trust numbers that DO appear
in say lines are the villager map (real, saturating) and village opinion
(pinned at 0, with phantom −3/−10 lines).

## Sense-test (read the transcripts, not just the counts)

The say corpus is capped at 200 lines per seed (days 1–13 to 1–21) — the
scenes below are the first three weeks; late-game lines are unobserved.

**Scene 1 — the village doesn't trust you yet (s190211, days 1–5).**
The first days are a wall of refusals: "Ask someone who trusts you."
"Why should I listen to you?" "You haven't earned that yet." "Not today.
Sorry. (Low trust — reluctant.)" Dozens of them, from named people
(Naoise, Elif, Mara, Omar) and from strangers described by their hands and
eyes. And every single day, the meal line: "Village meal: +1200 kcal, +1L
water from the communal pantry. (Half ration — they don't trust you yet.)"
Half rations on every observed day, on every seed — 0 full-ration meal
lines in the corpus. The gate is real and it bites for weeks even while
individual villagers warm to you fast. Coherent: the village as a body is
slower to trust than any one person in it.

**Scene 2 — the fire retells everything (s190211, day 11–12).**
"You catch fragments by the fire — Brayden telling Daniyar about you. The
truth left this story two fires ago. What's left is better theater." The
same story passes from Brayden to Hassan to Amara to Boris, picking up
decorations each time: "It's picking up decorations as it travels."
"Someone's added a flourish that wasn't there this morning." "Three
retellings in, nobody remembers what actually happened — but everyone has
opinions." The gossip lines are honest about what they are: the village
knows the story is drifting and enjoys it anyway. Coherent — and the
numbers back the theater: 41,639 hops, 7,726 mutations.

**Scene 3 — the moot (s190211, days 9–13).** "Word reaches you, days late:
Omar stands accused over what happened to Valeria. There's going to be a
moot. People are choosing sides." Then: "Jenny has been talking at the
fires. Enough voices — tonight they call a moot. Omar answers for what
happened to Valeria." "The moot turns to you. Your vote matters here — and
everyone will remember it." And then the scholar says nothing, and two days
later: "The moot doesn't wait forever. Two days of silence from the fire's
edge — the count is taken without you." "Asher counts on their fingers,
twice, like they don't trust the first number. Then, to the fire: '5 for
guilty. 1 against.'" The scene is coherent and a little damning: the game
promises "your vote matters" and the scholar never votes. The verdict lands
anyway.

**Scene 4 — small gifts, exact change (s190211, day 16–17).** "Donated
Muscadine Grapes (+540 kcal). Trust +1. They'll remember this." Then a
smaller gift: "Donated Lamb's Quarters (+100 kcal). Trust +0. They'll
remember this." A hundred calories earns literally nothing, and the line
says so plainly. Then the take-back: "They take back the trust your gift
earned. (Trust -1.)" "You took back what you gave. They noticed. Trust
-5." The gift economy is legible to the penny. Coherent.

**Scene 5 — the bond that lands (s190211, day 19).** A bond proposal
resolves in the telemetry: initiator gen_mk45w5p, target gen_thkzzvg,
accepted, chance 53, "relationship with inviter (+8)". A day-21 refusal:
chance 43, "relationship with inviter (+8); withdrawn temperament (−20);
joy goal (+10)". The reasons are character-shaped, not flat. Coherent.

**Sense verdict:** the trust/gossip surface reads as one coherent village.
Early distrust, half rations, fires full of drifting stories, moots that
proceed without you, gifts counted to the point. The incoherences are
specific and few: the phantom −3/−10 village-opinion lines, the moot's
"your vote matters" promise the scholar never keeps, and link raises that
are staged 172 times and answered never.

**Snapshot reconciliation:** 13,703 of 13,745 per-villager interval deltas
match the setter-event sums exactly. The 42 residue intervals (gap 506
points total) are all the same shape: a newcomer's trust key is created
mid-interval at 10–30 with no setter event yet (the key-creation write

roster trust at 5–20, plus same-interval bumps). The snapshots hold the
true values; only the first day's per-event attribution for 42 newcomers
is missing. Not a census gap.

**Wrap-vs-setter cross-check:** 21 bumpTrust calls with a nonzero applied
amount have no setter twin — the same new-key mechanism (the write creates
the key). tele('trust') fired 7,748 times vs 7,726 bumpTrust-attributed
setter events; the difference is those new-key writes.

**Limitations (recorded, not hidden):**

- Say corpus capped at 200 lines/seed (days 1–13 to 1–21). Late-game say
  lines are unobserved; the #2068 verdict rests on _evTrustAll = 0 across
  all 1,150 days, which is not capped.
- Hop distortion is per spreadGossip call, not per individual hop — a call
  that reaches several listeners shares one before/after distortion pair.
- `endConvo`'s recorded `exchangesBefore` is the villager's lifetime
  sitting count (1–24), not the sitting's exchanges — the instrument read
  `c.count` where the stipend reads `c.exchanges`. The stipend (+0 on all
  1,079 sittings, including early low-trust sittings where +1 would have
  applied) plus the talkRound code path is the proof of zero-exchange
  sittings, not the recorded field. Instrument fix owed for future arms.
- Cause labels `G` (82 events) and `eval` (6 events) are shallow: the
  classifier reads the first stack frame, which is often the `G.` alias
  wrapper or an array-callback frame; the semantic caller sits one frame
  deeper (e.g. conflictIncident, socialSimmer). Classified, not
  unattributed — but the label names the frame, not the system.
