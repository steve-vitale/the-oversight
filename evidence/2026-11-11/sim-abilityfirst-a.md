# sim-abilityfirst-a — abilities + XP first numbers (Loop A, fleshout-20261111T1015Z)

**Verdict: GO** — every pre-registered row passes (see GO table). This is a
first-numbers census under default competent play, seeds 190221–190228, 150-day
death-aware horizon. No lever, no landing claim, no confirm band spent.

**In plain words:** the village earns plenty of experience and hands out
plenty of magic — but almost all of it belongs to the villagers, not to
you, and almost none of it ever becomes a synergy. The scholar's six slots
fill up in the first two weeks on every run, so the slot ladder is an
opening beat, not a climb. The scholar casts the same five abilities over
and over (mostly shouting and raging in fights, and gardening magic in the
field), while villagers quietly collect skills and the occasional gift —
but almost never a fighting gift. The one synergy the scholar ever unlocked
in 851 village-days sat alone while villagers discovered synergies by the
dozen just by working side by side. And the dead keep handing the job to a
successor who starts over with nothing: the scholar dies 88 times across
the band, and every new bearer begins with empty slots and empty tracks.

Why the scholar earns so little: the scholar is ONE person doing
policy-driven deeds, while a dozen villagers forage, fight, heal and
teach all day — and every villager's deed credits the same table at the
same rate (parity holds: same deed, same XP). The scholar's per-track
levels at end are mostly 0–2 — but that number is misleading twice
over: the final bearer is usually a successor (74 mantle transitions),
so end-of-run scholar tracks describe whoever died last holding the
job, not the run's total learning. The honest scholar number is the
band total: 1,893 XP across ~88 bearers, ~21 XP per bearer-life.

Deed caps bind: 1,109 of 7,671 deed credits (14%) returned 0 on the
per-day cap — foraging (perDay 2), discovery (perDay 2), teaching
(perDay 1) hit their ceilings constantly. The cap is doing its job
(decisions, not grinding), and it is visible in the numbers.

The 62 "other:won" XP events are contest wins: `_contestEnd`
(contests.js:5266) credits track XP with why "won <Contest Name> on
camera" (Beastmaster ×3, Who Ate It? ×4, Duel, Gauntlet, The Pit…).
Classified, not unattributed — the analyzer's first-token label is
shallow (see metric review).

(need: 35). The devotion lane arms almost immediately — every

channel almost always finds resonance ≥35 first. And the "hums"

you hold 1. Not yet." — repeats daily on seeds where a fresh
successor holds no abilities to practice with. Channeling is a
real, daily-used system; its mastery lane is nearly vestigial
under competent play.
