## Item 13 — OLD-SAVES CORPSE CASES: LANDED (migration) + one held remainder

**The problem, plainly.** A promise you made to someone who later died is
supposed to be released — not kept, not broken, just unkeepable, with the
game saying so. That release happens when the person is removed from the
village. But a save written before that release existed — or a death whose
removal never ran it — keeps the promise OPEN in your journal forever,
pointing at a corpse. Old saves were never swept for this.

**What was built.** `Game.migrateDeadPromises()` (`src/js/game.js`, just
before `load()`), called at the very end of `load()` so a following save
persists fully-restored state. For every still-open promise (skipping your
own), it releases the promise when the holder is dead by evidence the save
itself carries: a `deathSeen` mark, a person-corpse naming them, or the
`deadMarked` record check. The journal row is updated in place (no duplicate
rows), and on release the load prints one plain summary line: "Promises
you made to [someone/people] who have since died were closed on this load
— released, not broken. Nothing is owed on them anymore." It saves only
when at least one promise was actually released, and it is idempotent
(released promises are skipped on the next load).

**Deliberate boundary, mirroring a live death exactly.** The favor ledger
is NOT touched (it stays `'promised'`), so the call-time backstop still
speaks for the favor itself — exactly as after a live death. Only the
journal promise is closed.

**Proof.** `scripts/test-gap-batch13-deadpromises.js`: **16/16 green.**
Cases: `deathSeen` mark releases exactly 1; person-corpse releases 1;
dead-marked roster record under true load conditions releases 1; guards —
living holder untouched, already-kept promise untouched even with a death
mark, your own promise untouched despite a stray mark; journal row reads
`'released'` after migration; favor ledger untouched; second migration
call releases 0.

**HELD remainder of item 13 (not built, and why).** Load-time closure of
open moot cases / doubts naming a dead accused. Removal-time closures
already exist (ghost-trial closure in `betrayal.js`, `closeDoubtsForGone`
in `truth.js`). The live rule distinguishes death from exile — an EXILED
accused is still tried in absentia — and that distinction cannot be
reconstructed safely for every old save from persisted state alone (a
removed person with neither a death mark/corpse nor an exile record, e.g.
fled or pre-tracking removals, would be misclassified). An incorrect
mass-resolution would rewrite live moots. Pending applications need no
migration: the arrival refusal already closes them at runtime, and the
applicant filter gates future draws.

## Item 16(a) — FAVOR DELIVERY, r107 F1: CLOSED with proof + one small fix

**What r107 F1 was.** The journey-crisis probe wraps the favor action
during driven conversations and logs calls returning exactly "They are no
longer available to receive help." Across 24 driven conversations it
logged 0 entries — the tenth consecutive zero panel (r71 through r107).

**Finding: the mechanism works; the panel zero is a probe-population
artifact.** The probe only ever calls living conversation partners, who by
construction essentially never produce that line. And after a live death
the promise is released immediately (item 13's rule) with its own spoken
line, so the journal never sits open-with-a-dead-person after a live
death. The unavailable line is a backstop for favor-ledger-without-release
cases (e.g. stale old saves — item 13's target).

**Proof.** `scripts/test-gap-batch16-f1.js`: **14/14 green.** Stages: a
living food-share favor passes the availability gate with no unavailable
line; the same favor after a real removal returns exactly "They are no
longer available to receive help.", with the promise released and the
journal row `'released'`; an away partner gets the road line (see fix);
delegated kinds return their own unavailable wording, not the generic line
(forage_help: "no longer available for this outing"; water_haul and
stay_close: their own reasons) — which is why an exact-string probe on
the generic line alone also misses delegated kinds; a constructed stale
old-save state (dead-marked, off-roster, promise still open, favor still
`'promised'`) keeps the journal row OPEN and names the unavailable cause
— the pre-migration baseline item 13 fixes.

**Fix landed in code (the broken half).** In `convoFavorAction`
(`src/js/convo-wants.js`, generic path only), after the person/roster/dead
check and before the node/adjacency check: if the partner is away on the
road, return "They are out on the road, miles from here. Find them when
they are back." Before the fix, the away case fell through to "Move next
to them, on the same side of the Haven door, to help" — telling the
player to step next to someone miles out on the road. Readiness stays
false either way; only the wording changes. The one existing test pinning
the old movement line stubs `convoFavorAction` with a fixture, so it is
unaffected. Delegated kinds' away wording was deliberately left unchanged
(their movement line is the same pattern, but changing shared adjacency
helpers risks the forage_together/end branches; the outing dawn note
already speaks for the dawn case) — observed, not changed.

**Proposal for the coordinator, not a decision:** whether the r107 probe
wrap itself is retired now that the mechanism is proven end to end and the
panel zero is explained. This worker does not retire it.

## Item 16(b) — FAVOR HAUL-RETENTION: mechanism PROVEN, wild half HELD

**The rule, plainly.** When you and a partner go out foraging together
for a promised favor and the partner is gone when you bring the haul
home, only YOUR share goes into the pantry. Your partner's share is lost
with them — the game never invents food nobody brought home.

**Mechanism proof.** `scripts/test-gap-batch16-haul.js`: **14/14 green.**
The retention rule lives in `src/js/favor-forage.js` (`end_without_partner`).
Case 1: partner gone via a real removal, your haul 2×100 kcal, partner's
virtual haul 3×100 kcal → the end-at-home step closes with 200 banked,
0 from the partner, the pantry growing by exactly 200, and the return line
naming the partner's share as lost. Case 2: partner gone + empty player
haul → still closes, 0 banked, no conjured food. Case 3 (control):
partner present → joint delivery banks both shares (200 + 300) and keeps
the promise. No source change was needed; the proof script is the landed
artifact, and it must not be read as claiming a wild observation.

**Wild nonzero observation: HELD, with the census.** No new sims were
run — re-running a full journey-social panel was judged disproportionate
and CPU-contended with sibling arms. The existing outing corpora were
censused directly:

- `evidence/2026-11-02/r102/` (journey-social r102, 4 seeds): 13 favor
  end states, 7 started, 5 delivered > 0 — every delivered case equals
  player + partner haul exactly (e.g. 1365+1445=2810). Zero partner-gone
  endings.
- `evidence/2026-11-02/r106/` (journey-social r106, 4 seeds × 140d): 5
  favor end states, 1 started + delivered (50+75=125, both shares).
  Zero partner-gone endings. The r106 analyzer also found zero
  deliver/end-blocked favors across all 4 seeds — the block category
  itself is stale post-r81-fix, since partner-gone endings are now
  allowed via the end step.

Combined: 18 favor ends, 8 started outings, 6 joint deliveries, 0
partner-gone endings. **Why the wild case never appears:** the chain
needs a favor requested + promised + outing started + haul gathered +
partner dies or goes away inside the 1–2 day meet-to-due window + the
player returns home and ends. Even started outings are rare (8/18 ends);
a partner death inside that exact window is rarer still. The mechanism
is proven; the wild sighting is a matter of waiting for that rare chain,
not of fixing code.

## Item 17 — NARRATION + DECISION: LANDED (two lines + one documented decision)

**Interpretation caveat, stated plainly.** The exact audit phrasing for
"night-out" and "pack-full refusal" was NOT found in the repo — no
`nightOut`/`night_out` identifier exists, and greps over the November 2
and 3 evidence notes for "night out"/"pack full" found no matching audit
item. What follows is this worker's best engineering reading from code;
the coordinator may redirect it if the audit meant a narrower spot. This
reading is NOT presented as confirmed audit fact.

**Night-out line (built).** In `sleep()`'s wake line (`src/js/game.js`),
when you wake NOT at Haven, the line now appends: "You spent the night
out here, away from Haven — and you're still out here at dawn." Before,
the wake line reported health and rest ("You wake rested… By the fire.
Good enough." / "Exposed. You'll wake stiff.") but never said in plain
words that the night was spent out, away from home — the first thing a
player out on a trip needs to know at dawn. A night in the hall or a bunk
gets no such line; a night in a tent pitched on Haven grounds (a Haven
tile) correctly gets none either.

**Pack-full refusal line (built).** In `performForageFavor`'s
`forage_together` failure branch (`src/js/favor-forage.js`), the return
line used to hedge: "Nothing came of that press. The patch, or your
pack, said no." — leaving the player unsure which problem to fix. It now
names the actual refusal, discriminated by whether the pack can carry
one more cell's take (~0.4 kg): a full pack gets "Nothing came of that
press — your pack is full, so nothing from this patch could fit. Eat
something, or leave some for the woods, and press again."; room in the
pack but an empty patch gets
"Nothing came of that press. This patch has nothing left to give right
now — walk to another green patch." Mechanics unchanged (no haul
recorded, still saves); the stale comment claiming the solo press "said
why" is corrected, since the favor dialogue shows this return line, not
the solo press's line. Both new lines keep the old "Nothing came of
that press" prefix on purpose: journey-deepening drivers count that
substring as the press-failed bucket, and the discriminator after it is
what names the refusal for the player. (The solo press itself already speaks pack-full
clearly, as do water fill, corpse loot, logs, tent, monster loot, and
harvest deferral — the gap was the favor dialogue's vague echo.)

**Silent-armor-craft DECISION (decided, no behavior change).**
NPC armor crafting STAYS SILENT when you are away from Haven, and speaks
when you are home. The decision rests on standing intent, not a new
rule: (1) Steve's "If you don't know, it doesn't show"
(`docs/DIRECTIVES.md`); (2) the established presence gate
(`src/js/game.js` `home_narration_presence`: village lives, ambient
social, fireside teaching narrate only when you are at Haven; the sim
still runs, and home deaths queue to away-news); (3)
`villagerArmorCraftDay` (`src/js/villager-build.js`) already gates BOTH
its lines on being home — success ("spent the afternoon at the workbench
and sewed a [piece] that holds together. [Name] wears it now.") and
failure ("spent the afternoon sewing, and the [piece] came apart in their
hands. The bark strips are wasted."). The no-silent-actions rule is
satisfied, not violated: the action's RESULT stays visible (the piece
enters spares, the spares flow moves it to whoever gains most, the
villager wears it, and the telemetry event records it) — only the
unwitnessable moment stays silent. Player-performed crafts always speak,
which is the correct contrast. A durable comment at the gate records
this decision so a later worker does not "fix" it.

**Proof.** `scripts/test-gap-batch17-narration.js`: **17/17 green.**
Wake line at a wild tile (ground quality) contains the night-out
sentence, and at Haven (hall) does not; favor joint-press failure with a
full pack returns the pack-full line, and with room but an empty patch
returns the patch line; armor witness gating — at home an attempt speaks
on either outcome (both outcomes have lines, so the RNG outcome does not
matter; the attempt is confirmed via the cooldown stamp plus a
sewing/workbench line), while away an attempt records the cooldown but
says no armor-craft line.
