## The question (finding #2599 stays OPEN on the draft-miss population)

The PRESENCE draft (reverted after NO-GO; its code lived in `game.js` `_presenceDraftLead`, called from `villagerMonsterTick`) fires when a living armed holder is in earshot of a wave-4+ launch — but holders exist at only ~8–13% of launches, are sheltered in 16/16 present cases (holdersupply §6), are NEVER on the monster's tile at tick time (`poolOnTile` 0 in 12,529 tick rows, presence-a §12), and presence-a counted ~21 distinct draft-miss day|mid pairs. The prior draftmiss arm (2026-11-20) dissolved most of those 21 pairs from the OLD corpus — 7 proven raid fights, 2 sheltered holders, 12 unresolved leaning raid/sheltered — and went NO-GO because the corpus could not separate raid fights from tick fights (`fightOrigin` mislabels raids as `world-tick`) and could not prove shelter. Its §6 names the follow-up this arm IS: a recorder that logs the raid itself and classifies at LAUNCH time with live reads, so the raid-vs-tick and shelter questions are answered at the source instead of inferred from tick rows.

This arm measures the launch-level population on a FRESH band: for EVERY unarmed-lead wave-4+ launch, what is the nearest living armed holder doing, how far away is it, and why did the draft (had it been on) not put the holder in front? The unit is the LAUNCH (distinct seed|day|mid|lead), with the distinct day|mid PAIR count reported alongside for presence-a comparability — collapsing the 4-rows-per-fight tick inflation presence-a §4 found.

## Instrument (frozen design — read-only, no game-code change)

- **Driver:** `scripts/draftmiss-driver-a.js` (clone of `scripts/lendpool-driver-a.js` — all cell / searcaster census + grant wraps + lead-rule + flee-cause + `v4tickPresence` + `v4lendPool` + `v4nearMiss` instrumentation kept verbatim — PLUS the `v4draftMiss` recorder below). The `v4lendPool` recorder is kept and must re-verify the pool on the fresh band. The `v4lends` recorder is kept but must stay EMPTY (lend OFF). **No `src/js` change.** If a recorder hook in `src/js` proves unavoidable, it is env-gated, reverted before commit, and said so in the evidence.
- **`v4draftMiss` recorder** (one row per unarmed-lead wave-4+ fight launch that becomes a real fight — outcome vKill | mFlee | vFlee | vDie — same fight filter as `v4nearMiss`): wraps `Game.resolveWildMonsterEncounter`, evaluates BEFORE the original runs, pure reads, zero `Math.random()` calls, zero game-state writes. Row fields: day / seed / mid / wave / lead / leadKind (`gun` = lead has no searcaster, `cells` = lead carries an empty searcaster) / raid / cause (frozen taxonomy below) / subreason / nearestHolder / nearestDist (Chebyshev tiles, null when no living holder) / nearestState (the lendpool frozen state classes) / nearestHealth / nearestCells / livingHolders / anyArmedEarshot / anyEligibleEarshot / lendable (the `lendPoolMoment` verdict, cross-checked against `v4lendPool`) / tickPooled (holder vids from the latest `v4tickPresence` row for the same day|mid, null when no tick row exists) / tickStale (bool, see below) / wouldFire (cause is `on-tile` or `adjacent-eligible` — the draft's maximum reach on this band).
- **`draftMissMoment(Game, vid, m, opts, near)`** — the pure launch-time classifier (exported for the proof test). Inputs are the live game state at launch plus the `nearMissMoment` result. It scans ALL living armed holders (searcaster + ≥1 cell, roster, never the scholar, never the lead) with their distance, state (`classifyHolderState`), health, and cells; the nearest (roster order on ties) decides the cause by the frozen priority order below.
- **Cause taxonomy (frozen, priority order — first match wins).** The task's list, plus three named additions marked [ADD] (each is a draft-filter or launch-path fact the task's list leaves unnamed; without them the honest cases would fall into `other` and the breakdown would lie):
  1. `raid` — the launch is raid-routed (`opts.raid`). The draft lives in `villagerMonsterTick`; the raid's defender draft never consults it (prior draftmiss arm §4/§8). Raid rows exist for coverage and are EXCLUDED from the non-raid breakdown denominators (the lendpool raid handling, verbatim). A raid row is classified, not unclassified.
  2. `stale-pre-tick-read` — the latest tick-pool row for the same (day, mid) disagrees with the launch-time read: (a) the pooled holder(s) are GONE at launch time (none of the pooled vids is a launch-time living armed holder — died, or lost the gun, between the pre-pass and the launch; within a call the only mechanism is death in an earlier monster's fight), OR (b) the tick pool is empty but a launch-time ELIGIBLE holder is in earshot. The launch-time cause is preserved on the row as `underlyingCause`. The prior arm measured (c) = 0 on the old corpus; this is the live-read test of that zero.
  3. `no-living-holder` — no living armed holder exists anywhere in the village (livingHolderCount 0).
  4. `sheltered-holder` — the nearest holder is sheltered (`Game.isSheltered`); nearestDist recorded (the holdersupply 16/16 shape, now with distance).
  5. `post-flee-resting` — the nearest holder is post-flee resting (`Game.isPostFleeResting`).
  6. `low-health` [ADD] — the nearest holder's village health ≤ 20. The draft refuses these holders (presence-a §1); the task's list has no name for them.
  7. `on-tile` — the nearest holder is ON the monster's tile (dist 0) AND eligible (armed, health > 20, not sheltered, not resting). The draft would have fired; the miss is the launch path / PRESENCE off. Presence-a measured `poolOnTile` 0 in 12,529 tick rows — this is the launch-level test of that zero.
  8. `adjacent-but-unreachable` — the nearest holder is in earshot (dist 1) but on-expedition (`agencyState().exped`) or away (`village.away`) — adjacent by tile math, unreachable in fiction (the lendpool day-127 shape).
  9. `adjacent-eligible` [ADD] — the nearest holder is adjacent (dist 1), eligible, and NOT on-expedition/away. The draft would have fired. The task's list names `on-tile` (dist 0) but no dist-1 eligible case; folding it into `other` would hide the draft's reachable pool.
  10. `out-of-earshot` [ADD] — the nearest holder is dist ≥ 2 (any state; refusal reasons above win first, so this is the nearest holder otherwise eligible-or-away but too far, or on-expedition/away at dist ≥ 2). Nearest state + dist recorded for the cross-cut.
  11. `other` — residual, with a `subreason` string naming the situation (e.g. `eligible-adjacent-unfired` if the launch path somehow bypasses the tick — the draft lives only in the tick, so a would-fire holder at a non-tick launch lands here with the launch path named). A large `other` share is a diffuse verdict, not a hidden pass.

## Primary metric (BINDING, §14)

**DIAG-GO iff ALL hold:**

1. **Instrument validity — fixtures 100%.** `scripts/test-draftmiss-a.js` passes every assertion: the pinned corpus test (prior arm) still green; `draftMissMoment` attributes correctly on fixtures (every cause above, staged real, 0 draws, pure reads); the analyzer `--selftest` passes (new judge shapes incl. the classified-breakdown verdict, the stale-overlay rule, and the distinct-pair collapse).
2. **Coverage — the draft-miss is fully classified.** `v4draftMiss` rows exist for ≥ 95% of unarmed-lead (leadKind `gun` | `cells`) wave-4+ real fights in the corpus (denominator derived independently from `v4encounters`, same choke-point join as `nearMissStats`), and ≤ 5% of those rows carry an unclassified / `unknown` cause. Raid rows count toward coverage (they must exist for the join) and are excluded from the breakdown.
3. **The answer — quantified, with a named dominant cause and the next lever.** The evidence reports: the breakdown by cause (shares of all non-raid unarmed-lead launches) and by nearestDist buckets; the distinct-pair count alongside the distinct-launch count; the earshot base rate over ALL non-raid wave-4+ launches (armed and eligible, separately); the would-fire bound (`on-tile` + `adjacent-eligible`); the tick-stale count; and names the dominant cause — the largest single cause class, with its share — plus the named next lever with its premise stated. If the largest class holds < 30%, the evidence says so plainly ("no single dominant cause — diffuse") and names the top two classes instead; the bar is the quantified answer, not a particular cause.

**Verdict ladder (frozen):** DIAG-GO = all three hold → the diagnosis lands (instrument + evidence; no game-code change to land). DIAG-NO-GO = any bar misses → the diagnosis is not established (instrument failure = SUBSTRATE finding; coverage failure = the recorder missed launches — diagnose, do not ship the breakdown). A DIAG-GO with a diffuse breakdown (no class ≥ 30%) still names the next lever, but the lever's premise must say the pool is diffuse, not single-cause. This arm changes no game behavior, so there is no guardrail bar on game outcomes; the guardrail is instrument purity — `v4lends` empty, `v4lendPool` re-verified (near-miss lendable count agrees with pool moments), zero draws added (proven by the fixtures).
