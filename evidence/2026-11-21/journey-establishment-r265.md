### FIX 2 — BEARER-END (`src/js/favor-forage.js`, `src/js/favor-water.js`)
r81 fixed the partner-death hang; the mirror case — bearer-death — was never fixed. When the run's bearer is dead or roster-gone, the successor may now end the run at Haven, honestly: the successor unloads only what they carry (the dead bearer's haul is lost with them), the partner's share stays with the partner (no conjured food or water), the promise is renounced, the favor goes 'broken', the run closes. A living previous bearer still owns the outing (work AND end stay blocked). Proof: `scripts/test-bearer-end-r265.js`, **33/33 GREEN**. Live: no opportunity — no run stranded on the panel (see census, 101970). The driver routes a dead-bearer stranded run to the honest end; the wedge logged zero stranded runs, so the close was never attempted live. Mechanism carried by the proof test.
