## Determinism drift (major finding — read this first)
The sim is deterministic for a fixed seed order and config, but outcomes are **position-dependent within one process** — a "seed" is not an independent draw of its nominal seed.

Seed 109301 (first in both configs) is byte-identical across configs.
- Conclusion: per-seed RNG/state isolation leaks across seeds in one process. Likely shape: a module capturing `Math.random` at first load, or module-level mutable state carrying over — the mechanism is NOT yet proven; do not assert a specific cause. Panels remain comparable across runs with identical seed order/config (which is why r269/r272/r275 comparisons hold), but cross-config seed comparisons do not measure the seed alone. Do not fix the harness mid-panel — that would confound the panel. Filed as a driver-arm item: isolate per-seed state (fresh process per seed, or reset module state between seeds) before the next band judges cross-config comparisons.
