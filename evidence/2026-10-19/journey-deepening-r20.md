## Design notes for Steve (his calls, not levers)
1. **The kills-only gate works as designed for competent play** — 3/4 and 4/4 unlock rates, no farming needed. The one miss was contact variance (same seed unlocked under patrols). No gate change indicated by this run.
2. **The roster price is the actual wall:** unlock seeds lose 13–34 villagers, nearly all to combat. If wave 4's gate is also kills-gated (r17 tablerecon: 4 wave-3 ledger points), the compounding roster cost deserves a look — the §14c rule exists because the scholar survives while the village dies around them.
3. **The apex is nearly decorative:** moderator (wave-2 apex) was faced on 4 seeds and killed on 0; grief counselor / performance review likewise faced-but-never-killed. The gate is satisfiable on soft targets — but deed-seeking proved the mid-tier (Static Kite, Union Rep) IS killable when the player seeks fights. Whether the apex should stay unkillable-by-policy is a design call.
4. **Contact variance is the hidden gate:** competent 9304's 94 days with 1 wave-2 encounter means a real player can play a full deepening window and barely meet the middle fauna. If wave-2 contact is meant to be the leg's texture, the encounter scheduling — not the kill gate — is the lever.
