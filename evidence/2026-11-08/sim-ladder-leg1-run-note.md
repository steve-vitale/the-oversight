## Design calls made in leg 1 (for leg 2 / the panel)

- `counted` is what counts toward the NEXT rung (the number the bar
  fills), capped at the next rung's threshold. `villageLadder()`
  also returns `cap`, `countedTracks`, `detail` (ledger total, summed
  XP, link/contest/breadth counts) for leg 2's voicing.
- Stage is LIVE (derived each call): deaths can drop the counted
  total below a rung. `peakStage` (monotonic marker) remembers the
  climb; `ladderStage()` returns the live stage, `ladderPeakStage()`
  the peak. Leg 2's seat gate decides which to read.
- Stage 4 (Galactic Seat) is never returned by `ladderStage()` — it
  is the capstone delivery, recorded on `progState().ladderCapstone`
  by leg 2.
- No ratchet on counted points (unlike Standing's peak ratchet) —
  the ladder is the LIVING village's strength.
- Beat text is new System voice ([needs-eyes] on the ladder commit).
