## The 53008 mechanism, in plain words

On the old master, 53008 was one wave-3 monster short of the lesser seat: it had faced two distinct wave-3 monsters in 150 days, and the table never came — Arc IV never fired, the seat stayed none, the sit drive skipped `not-announced` 448 times. On current master the same village faces its third wave-3 monster on day 98 — the spool, fought by the player character — and everything moves at once: the seat prices lesser on day 98, the table is announced on day 99 ("The table is being set — a lesser seat, priced for what you built"), and the village sits that same day, standing 199, with one wave-4 kill behind it. A day earlier, on day 90, the village heard the other side of the story: "THE TABLE — the ring of pale light flickers at the edge of the sky... and does not come. Not yet." The table waited eight days for one more monster, and the monster came. The pellet passes through four hands along the way (player → Valeria → Raj → Inas), the village loses five people to the wild and the arena, and still sits. The blocker was never the sitting — it was the third wave-3 face, and it arrived one fight late.

**What moved it between masters:** the only live game-code change in `6680d7924..6e69df4ef` is the pellet radiation rework (feastBuff.js, game.js +13/−, statusEffects.js, data; the tenddrive fix in the same range was reverted, net zero). The rework changes the seeded RNG stream, which reshuffles encounter draws — 53008's spool fight on d98 is a reshuffle outcome, not a designed seat effect. Stated as the likely mechanism, not proven: a bisect would prove it, and it is not worth a bisect — the seat works, and the reshuffle moved the seed in the sitting direction. The honest statement is that frozen-band roots decay with every src change, and this run re-verified them rather than trusting them.
