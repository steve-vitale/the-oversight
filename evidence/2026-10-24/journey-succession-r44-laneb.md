## Headline

close is now named precisely. The old echo filter was missing real
memorials: on every seed the widened filter recovers exactly the lines
the tele count saw, minus only the funeral-arc lines it means to exclude

genuine mention of the dead. The missing mechanism has a name: at about
day +3 the village raises a marker by the fire — "The village raises a
marker by the fire — [name], day [N]. [cause]. A place to stand." — a
narration line, not villager talk, so the old format filter could never
see it. The one chain death in the run lands with its cause from both
lists. And the office-entry writers question sharpens rather than
closing: across 8 movement days, NOT ONE carries a phoenix, broadcast,
or gift line — the known writers explain zero of this run's movement.
What does show up on movement days is public talk (WORD TRAVELS
Beloved/Feared lines), crisis lines, and generic System marks — a
candidate channel, not a proven writer.

## The collapsing-village pattern (a finding, not a complaint)

The two scripted early-kill seeds arrived at their deaths with full
shelves (pantry 25,289 and 23,250 at the death). The three organic
standard-regime seeds arrived with 0, 5,840, and 5,850 kcal — forty
days of competent play had spent the pantry down, and the death
then landed on a village already running on empty. 25003 ended in
2 days, 25005 in 8, 25004 in 27. Whether forty days of competent
play SHOULD drain the pantry this far is a food-economy question
for those loops, named here, not answered here: the succession leg
only establishes that the death lands differently on a fed village
than on a starving one, and that a starving village's successor
month is mostly a wake.
