## What is measured

Actual harvested crop cycles carry gardenShares: ordinary/assisted or
legacy/legacyAssisted. Assistance is an applied cycle flag, not the crop's
counterfactual incremental benefit or the current holder's class. Producer
identity remains independently tracked. NPC blends use actual rounded delivered
calories; player output tags physical species units before the cycle resets.
Labels, prior holdings and old food never acquire invented origin.

Transfers, pantry splitting/merging, rations/theft, unknown-lump staging/sorting,
processing, calorie-weighted mixed recipes and real refunds retain shares.
Unclassified portions remain in the denominator. Existing settled physical
meal receipts supply gardenIntakeTotals v1, with primary nourishment, sources
and player/NPC roles. It begins when first recorded and persists beyond the
old eight-day ring. The old all-food metric's23-metric contract is unchanged.

The passive sidecar separately records nominal covered stocks, removals in the
real spoilage sweep, reserve debits BEFORE clock settlement, NPC hunger
equivalents, nominal seed inputs and requested/elapsed world ticks. It does
not price ordinary metabolism or duty opportunity costs. Unknown origin is
not non-garden food; nominal stocks are not edible security. Diagnostic
unallocated nominal output includes unclassified transformations/exports or
losses. It must not be called waste or a closed net-profit account.
