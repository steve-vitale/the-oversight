## The fix: the dead kit clause (r12's orientation beat, half-dead)

**Root cause** (proven on 4/4 r36 seeds): the orientation line's kit clause
read `(s.backgroundAbilities || [])` — but playerDeath sets
`s.backgroundAbilities = []` unconditionally two dozen lines above (ability
lanes 2026-10-14: occupations grant SKILLS only, never slots). The clause
could never fire. The handoff named the new face (age, occupation) but never
their hands — the build change stayed silent in the fiction.

**Fix** (src/js/ledger.js, +9/-1): the kit clause now reads `s.abilities` —
the heir's real kit, replaced by `mantleBecome` above with the successor's
own abilities (Steve 2026-10-14: "You become that villager"). Post-fix line:
"🪞 You wake up somebody else. Ernestine Webb, 34 — an appliance repair
tech. Her hands know different work now: Beast-calm."

**Proof:** `scripts/test-succession-orientation-20261014.js` (updated in the
same commit — kit source moved from backgroundAbilities to
scholar.abilities, per the green-list rule): RED-verified 2 FAILURES on
pre-fix code via patch-revert (heirs held Beast-calm; the line never named
it), ALL GREEN post-fix. This fills finding (d): the fiction now names the
new kit at the handoff, so the fall is visible — the player hears what the
new hands know against what they just lost.

**Regression:** `check.js --only succession` 11/11 passed (19s) on the fixed

## Instrument finding: the agency bonus (design, working as intended)

The r36 pilot initially "mispredicted" the successor (Piper trust 91 vs
Lucas 90 — engine picked Lucas). Root cause, proven via trust-write tracing:
`villager-agency.js` wraps `playerDeath` and grants
`min(12, floor(agencyScore/4))` trust to every living villager BEFORE the
trust-sorted pick — "the village's choice of successor naturally favors the
explorer who became someone." Lucas (who'd earned his own abilities)
got +2 → 92 > 91. The instrument now replicates the bonus (predHit 4/4).
Design note, not a bug: the village prefers heirs with their own agency —
which is why every r36 heir stepped up with a real kit of their own rather
than an empty one. This softens the fall's harshness by design: the office
goes to someone who became someone.
