## The defect (finding #2508, from sim-notick-a)

`classifySeed`'s churn block detects a holder death only from census
`holderAlive=false` and daily-roster `dead:true` — but dead villagers DROP
OUT of the daily roster, and census rows stop when the plan is deleted on
death. Both fallbacks therefore miss most deaths, and the instance closes
`plan-deleted` instead of `holder-died`. sim-notick-a found two cases
(david_kim d86, gen_4hoo6rr d147) because its tracer reads holder death
from the `deaths` array (kind `villager`) first. Any arm judging a plan's
end by closeKind alone inherits the mislabel.

The churn block is copied verbatim across six analyzers — there is no

## The fix (frozen)

In each churn block, consult `j.deaths` FIRST: the earliest entry with
`kind === 'villager'`, `who === <holder vid>`, `day >= <instance seedDay>`
is the holder-death day; it sets `closeDay`/`closeKind = 'holder-died'`.
Only if the deaths array yields nothing do the census `holderAlive=false`
scan and the daily-roster `dead:true` backup run, exactly as today.
Nothing else in the block changes: retargeted closes (set by the seed
state machine) are untouched, the `plan-deleted` branch (census ends
before the horizon, holder alive) is untouched, and the
`diedDay >= seedDay` guard stays.
