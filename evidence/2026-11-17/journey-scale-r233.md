  It sums the ladder's `fightsByWave['4']` (59 band-wide); the kill
  count lives in the scholarcount `kAttr.w4total` (103401: 3 kills

  fights" and add a band kill total from kAttr — the verdicts don't
  change (they ride on slain titans), but the number stops
  overstating.

  DAYCAP 140 means a healthy band reads ~123 (+3.4%, HOLD). The
  control still works as a collapse detector (103403's 54-day band
  would have dragged a 4-seed mean down), but on an 8-seed panel it
  should be re-based on recent 8-seed bands, not carried from the
  4-seed rolling mean. Propose: roll it forward with this band's 123.
- **The refusal-spam bar (≥10) caught the shape at exactly 10 on
  103402** — one more hearing and it would have missed a real
  6-refusals-in-a-day seed (103401). The bar measures the worst day,
  which is the right shape (a day is what the player feels), but the
  threshold is arbitrary; keep it, and keep the per-seed worst-day
  line in the output.
- **The tableActive bar (≥7 present-held rows) caught 103405 at 78**
  — an order of magnitude over. No threshold doubt there.
