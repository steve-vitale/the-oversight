# FANS — the audience gets faces (Steve 2026-10-11)

The galactic character scaffold (docs/CANON.md) runs personas through
shows → producers → aliens → table. This doc covers the other half:
the AUDIENCE. Favor, viewership, and fan clubs are currently stats.
Stats don't get cheered for. People do.

## Canon

- The audience is composed of **named, recurring alien fan personas** —
  individuals with species flavor, lane affinities, personalities, and
  arcs. They are the ones watching.
- A fan persona has: name, species/homeworld sketch, lane affinity
  (which contest lanes they love), personality (what they cheer/boo),
  and an arc (how their feeling about the PLAYER evolves across the run).
- They appear in: show commentary (named reactions), care package notes
  (signed, personal — not generic loot), fan club figureheads (each
  per-lane club gets a face), and eventually at the galactic table.
- **Knowledge-gated like the personas**: a fan is "a viewer" / "the
  audience" until introduced. If you don't know them, they don't show.
- Fans are NOT fighters. They never enter combat. Their power is
  attention: Favor, votes, packages, and — at the table — testimony.
- The System name-drops fans obliviously ("Oh! Zyx is CRYING! They
  never cry! Well. They cried at the broth thing too.").

## Rules

- 4–6 fan personas per run, drawn from a larger pool — never the same
  six twice. No repeated names within a run (unique-person law).
- Care package notes are SIGNED and personal. A package from a fan
  who watched you starve reads differently from one who watched you win.
- Fan arcs respond to the player's real history: a fan who loved your
  pilot episode and watched you go dark should feel betrayed, not
  just decrement.
- Never let fans become a management screen. They are faces in the
  crowd and voices in the commentary, not resources to allocate.
