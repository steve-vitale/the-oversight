# KIN — The Taking

Steve's canon (2026-10-11), in his words. Read before touching anything about
missing relatives, the disappeared half, stasis kids, or the table's kid-return.

## The Taking happened at the Scattering — day zero

Half of all adults were taken by the System IMMEDIATELY, at the Scattering
itself — not after the first week, not at day 7. Any dialog claiming the
disappearance happened "after the first week" is wrong canon (it contradicted
the player experience: nobody sees it happen in week one).

The System took the boring half. The villages are the entertaining remainder.
The System's line: "FUN FACT! Half of humanity is GONE! We took them! Right
at the START! The boring half — the sitters, the yawn-ers!"

## The horror is personal

Every villager's missing relatives were probably in the boring half.
"Your relatives were probably in it! Statistics! Don't think about it!"
Villager kin/backstory beats may reference taken family — the taking is always
AT THE START, never later.

## The taken adults: cheerfully vague, never confirmed

The System will not say whether the taken are dead or alive. Its line:
"They're somewhere SAFE! Somewhere BORING! Like THEM!" The not-knowing is the
horror. No code, dialog, or Codex entry may confirm their fate either way.

## The kids are in stasis

The System took the taken half's children too — into stasis. Its line:
"Can't have little ones running around without their boring parents! They're
NAPPING! Very cozy! Very still!"

Children of KEPT adults (villagers who remained) stayed with the villages and
grew up normally. The stasis kids are specifically the taken half's children.

## Getting the kids back: the good galactic table path

Returning the stasis kids is reserved for the GOOD galactic table path. It is
not achievable by any other means — no quest, no ability, no bargain
short of the table returns them. The table-outcome build must honor this:
the kids' return is a good-path table outcome, full stop.
