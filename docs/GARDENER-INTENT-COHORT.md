# Next bounded milestone: deliberate garden investment

Owner: Codex policy/measurement. Muse owns numerical cultivation calibration.
Read GARDENS.md, SIMULATING.md and current garden-opportunity-audit-r2 first.
Question: can a character choosing a garden build make useful use of a growing
Haven, while ordinary gardening remains slow and modest?

## Current prototype (pending review and ship-loop intake)

`scripts/policies/gardener-intent.js` declares four mixed-survival profiles:
garden-control, garden-invest, garden-control-expand, garden-invest-expand.
All four resolve midday sheets with the capable brain. Thus garden-control is
a declared methodology control, not the unchanged stock capable policy. The
investment arms prioritize actual unheld garden offers and preserve core build
pieces at earned replacement decisions. Held power levels grow through actual
use; taking duplicate powers is not an invented upgrade mechanic.

Expansion arms allow one new-bed attempt per bearer/day at health60+,1800+kcal
and no later than tick320. They query the actual make gate, require known seed
value and available pantry space, use existing empty/dead beds first, and stop
at Haven capacity. They defer at three days of identified, finished, unspoiled,
safe stored food, or when projected crop flow plus the usable buffer covers a
declared2400kcal/person/day planning demand. Nominal pantry occupancy is used
only for space; unsafe/unknown/raw food does not become an edible reserve.
The two-bed practice allowance applies only to held core powers belowL3.
Projected crops use only owned magic and known plant values; the estimate is
not actual production, edible intake, a free cast or a balance guarantee.

These thresholds were recorded in gardener-intent-preregister-r1.json before
reading outcomes. The new run uses600 iterations for all16 matched screening
seeds plus two separately listed extended seeds per arm, with one actual on/off
control per arm. It remains a16-seed screening, not64 confirmation seeds. The
stock four-policy64-case context panel is separately recollected to prove the
new optional hooks preserve their complete rows, Game hashes and all23 metrics.

## Controller contract

Use one unchanged Game and shared observer/controller revisions for every arm.
Declare intent explicitly; do not describe the existing mixed policies as this
cohort. Obtain powers only from real offered sheets; distinguish arrival offers,
earned track choices, upgrades, inherited kits and later class recognition.
Audit every offer-resolution boundary, including choices opened inside daily
actions: an initial priority hook does not prove later choices follow intent.
No forced powers, class labels, seed knowledge, materials, food or filled beds.

Replace the four-bed ceiling only in the declared expansion arm. Query actual
public gates when the policy considers building; record reasons to expand or
defer. Weigh useful food demand and existing ripe/growing crops against planting
cost, time, seed availability, reserve, storage and learning/mastery. Idle empty
capacity is acceptable. Do not make max beds the objective or silently remove
maintenance, harvest, processing, donation or eating costs. Use actual actions,
not direct inventory/plot edits. Preserve state/RNG neutrality of observation.

Observe NPCs separately. A held power or named Gardener class does not prove a
particular resident is present, healthy, free and assigned when work is due.
Do not change autonomous NPC mechanics as a shortcut to making player intent
look viable. Any later caretaker-selection policy is a separate methodology arm.

## Experiment and done-when

Pre-register policy decisions and explicit thresholds before inspecting wins.
Start with16 matched seeds in a small factorial screen: earned garden priority
on/off and demand-aware expansion on/off, otherwise identical decision paths.
This separates prioritizing powers from using more beds. Keep normal shared
capable/maker/merchant/caregiver panels as context, not interchangeable controls.
Use64 seeds and independent holdouts before small-effect/general viability claims.

Retain actual endings for extended cases, including deaths and community loss;
report capped cases as incomplete. Observe acquisition-to-use and upper-tier
gate/build opportunities, actual labor/calorie costs, gross output, edible crop
intake/nourishment, spoilage, pantry/pack stocks and unknown/unallocated residual.
Review every all23 domain and individual policy; more wins cannot cancel harm.
Winning should remain difficult. Register any later Game lever separately.

Done means: real offer/blocked-expansion/action fixtures, source-stable raw
panels and genuine on/off controls, independent replays of changed cases,
documented tradeoffs/coverage limits, quick/full green, review, and shared
ship-loop intake before using the new policy as a calibration gate. No physical
phone claim follows from node simulations; any player-facing follow-up also
needs the actual touch/save/Continue/lifecycle proof appropriate to its changes.
