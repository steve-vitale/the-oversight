# Animal recording attributions — src/audio/animals/

All files below are real animal field recordings from Wikimedia Commons,
used as the game's animal-monster voices (Steve 2026-10-12: "Animals should
have actual animal noises wherever possible"). Converted to 128kbps MP3;
longer recordings trimmed to their loudest section for game use.

## boar/1.mp3 — Wild boar voice (Bulldozer)
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Domestic_pig_grunting.webm
- Description: "Domestic pig (Sus scrofa domesticus) grunting in Cuba."
- License: CC BY 2.0
- Note: domestic pig is the same species as wild boar (Sus scrofa).

## deer/1.mp3 — Deer voice (Highbeam Deer / Mirror Stag)
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fallow_Deer_(Dama_dama)_(W1CDR0001444_BD17).ogg
- Description: "A male Fallow Deer or 'Buck' bellowing in Haldon Forest, Devon, England"
- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
- Note: fallow deer (Dama dama), not white-tailed — an honest deer bellow.

- License: CC BY-SA 4.0
